# How To Train Your Dragon Unofficial

This website is a project created by a fan of the How To Train Your Dragon book series, films, and television shows.

## Project Overview

The objective of this site is to provide a comprehensive archive of the entire franchise created by Cressida Cowell and DreamWorks Animation. It serves as an intuitive library where fans can easily explore every book, film, and television series, alongside a list of characters and dragon species.


## Disclaimer

This is an unofficial fan website. I do not claim ownership of the How To Train Your Dragon franchise. 

All rights to the characters, stories, and media belong to the respective owners, including DreamWorks Animation and Cressida Cowell.
