Cressida Cowell

Books

How to Train Your Dragon (2003)

Young Hiccup Horrendous Haddock III must capture and train a dragon as part of his clan initiation—without being eaten in the process.

How to Be a Pirate (2004)

Hiccup and friends embark on a treasure hunt after discovering a riddle in a coffin at sea, searching for the treasure of Grimbeard the Ghastly.

How to Speak Dragonese (2005)

When Hiccup's friends are kidnapped, he learns he'll need to speak Dragonese to rescue them.

How to Cheat a Dragon's Curse (2006)

A strange curse threatens Toothless, and it's a race against time to fix it.

How to Twist a Dragon's Tale (2007)

With Berk in peril, Hiccup must steal a dragon treasure—posing as a villain to save his people.

A Hero's Guide to Deadly Dragons (2008)

Hiccup needs a new guide on how to be a hero in the face of deadly, fire-breathing foes.

How to Ride a Dragon's Storm (2009)

A thrilling dragon ride reveals secrets of the skies and dangerous adventures beyond Berk.

How to Break a Dragon's Heart (2009)

Hiccup must find a way to mend his dragon's broken spirit and save the day.

How to Steal a Dragon's Sword (2011)

Hiccup must retrieve the sword of King Harald to save his tribe while navigating betrayal and mysteries.

How to Seize a Dragon's Jewel (2012)

Amid a brewing dragon rebellion, Hiccup teams with unlikely allies to steal a magical jewel.

How to Betray a Dragon's Hero (2013)

Betrayal ripples through Berk as Hiccup confronts his loyalties in a tale of sacrifice and redemption.

How to Fight a Dragon's Fury (2015)

In the epic finale, dragons are enslaved and Berk is under siege. Hiccup faces impossible odds to save his world.

DreamWorks Animation

Films

How to Train Your Dragon (2010)

A Viking breaks all rules and befriends a dragon he is supposed to kill. He decides to call him Toothless and they join forces to bring peace.

How to Train Your Dragon 2 (2014)

Hiccup and Toothless explore unmapped lands and discover Hiccup's mother, while protecting Berk from the warlord Drago.

The Hidden World (2019)

Hiccup aims to unite Vikings and dragons, but must stop the ruthless Grimmel who plots to wipe out all dragons.

New

How to Train Your Dragon (2025)

A live-action remake of the beloved 2010 animated film, itself loosely based on the 2003 novel by Cressida Cowell.

DreamWorks Animation

Shows

Riders of Berk

Hiccup and friends lead the Berk Dragon Academy, learning to coexist with dragons and protect their village.

Defenders of Berk

The Dragon Academy faces greater threats, pushing Hiccup and his riders to become true defenders of their home.

Race to the Edge

Hiccup and Toothless lead the Dragon Riders beyond Berk to discover new lands, dragons, and enemies.

Rescue Riders

Twins Dak and Leyla spend their days rescuing dragons and helping people in their adopted town, Huttsgalor.

The Nine Realms

Set 1,300 years after the events of the original story, dragons are now just a legend to the modern world.

Short Films & Specials

Specials

Legend of the Boneknapper (2010)

Hiccup and his friends embark on an exciting journey in search of the legendary bone-clad dragon, the Boneknapper.

Book of Dragons (2011)

An encyclopedia of dragon species packed with fun facts and detailed lore about the dragons of Berk.

Gift of the Night Fury (2011)

Hiccup helps Toothless when the other dragons leave Berk ahead of the Viking winter holiday.

Dawn of the Dragon Racers (2014)

The untold origin story of how dragon racing first began on Berk.

Homecoming (2019)

Hiccup and Astrid discover that their children have developed a fear of dragons and set out to fix it.

Snoggletog Log (2019)

A cosy holiday animation of dragons settled by the fire — perfect for the festive season.

Thank You

A heartfelt thank you to Cressida Cowell for her wonderful books that sparked the world of How to Train Your Dragon, and to the entire team at DreamWorks Animation for bringing that world to life on screen.

From the first book to the twelfth, then soaring onto the big screen in 2010 and continuing through every show and film that followed — thank you for the adventure, the memories, and the dragons.
